\section{Introduction}
In today's highly competitive mobile gaming space, where more than 25,000 new games are released each month \cite{b15}, it is essential for games to have a steady stream of new content to keep players engaged. For these updates to reach users reliably, they must be deployed through a controlled, reproducible process. As shown in \cite{b14}, frequently-updated apps remain popular with users and tend to receive higher rankings. This paper presents a concrete implementation of such a pipeline via a Godot 4 (.NET/C\#) mobile game case study.

In the following sections the relevant technologies and terms used in the example project (such as Continuous Integration and the Google Play Store) are explained in detail. A case study then demonstrates how all of these technologies connect to produce a fully automated pipeline.
